The checklist

What I actually look at on a small business site.

Nine concrete things that decide whether a visitor calls you or leaves. I check all of them before quoting.

  1. Does it work with one thumb on a phone?
  2. Is the phone number tappable?
  3. Is there a WhatsApp or enquiry path?
  4. Does the page load in under three seconds?
  5. Is there a clear heading and description for Google?
  6. Do the forms actually deliver somewhere?
  7. Are opening hours and address current?
  8. Is the copyright year stale?
  9. Does Google get structured business data?

Questions

The things people ask before saying yes.

Why is this so much cheaper than an agency?

Because you are not paying for account managers, discovery workshops or a monthly retainer. The scope is small and fixed, and I work from a tested build process. You pay for the result, not the overhead.

Do I own the code and the design?

Yes. On payment you get the source files and full ownership. There is no lock-in and no licence fee.

What if I don't like the result?

You see a preview before paying. If it does not match the agreed brief, you owe nothing. If it does, one round of revisions is included.

Can you work with my existing website?

Usually yes. If your site is on WordPress or a standard host I can patch it, or rebuild the page and hand you the files to swap in. I will tell you honestly which is cheaper before you commit.

How do I pay?

UPI (furjack@slc or furjack@ybl) for clients in India, PayPal (paypal.me/fgmodss) for international clients. Payment is due after you approve the preview, before final files are handed over.

How fast can you start?

Usually the same day. Most one-page builds are delivered within 48 hours of receiving your content; rescues and automations within 72.
